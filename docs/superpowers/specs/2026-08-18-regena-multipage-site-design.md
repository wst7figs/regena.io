# Regena Multi-Page Website Design

Date: August 18, 2026  
Status: Approved direction, pending final written-spec review

## 1. Objective

Expand the approved Regena homepage into a coherent authority website for regenerative and longevity clinic owners. The site must make Regena feel like a premium managed patient-growth infrastructure partner rather than an AI tool vendor or general marketing agency.

The website has three commercial jobs:

1. Help a clinic recognize where its patient-growth system is failing.
2. Route the clinic toward the appropriate Regena solution.
3. Convert qualified organic visitors into strategy calls.

The build is design-complete with strong draft copy. Real calendar, CRM, analytics, form transport, final team photography, counsel-approved legal language, and final VisionMax media will be connected later.

## 2. Audience and Positioning

The primary audience is owners and operators of privately owned regenerative and longevity clinics. The site may acknowledge adjacent medical and aesthetic clinics, but the public front door remains narrow.

Regena is positioned as managed patient-growth infrastructure. It connects patient demand, response, qualification, scheduling, follow-up, enrollment visibility, and ongoing optimization.

The site must not position Regena as:

- A self-serve SaaS product.
- A generic AI receptionist.
- A broad automation agency.
- A conventional advertising agency.
- A guarantee of revenue, medical outcomes, rankings, or advertising approval.

## 3. Approved Information Architecture

The initial site contains these routes:

- `/` — Home
- `/solutions` — Solutions hub and comparison
- `/solutions/patient-conversion-system` — Patient Conversion System
- `/solutions/growth-partnership` — Regena Growth Partnership
- `/approach` — Our Approach
- `/clinics` — For Clinics
- `/outcomes` — Outcomes and client proof
- `/company` — Company and team
- `/book` — Qualification and booking experience
- `/privacy` — Privacy structure with draft legal content
- `/terms` — Terms structure with draft legal content

The initial build does not include an empty blog, fabricated specialty pages, fake customer logos, or separate capability pages. A dedicated VisionMax case study, Insights hub, specialty landing pages, security page, and additional case studies are phase-two additions after the required source material exists.

## 4. Navigation

The global navigation is:

- Solutions dropdown
- Our Approach
- For Clinics
- Outcomes
- Company
- Book a Strategy Call

The Solutions dropdown contains two equal-sized solution entries and a comparison link:

### Patient Conversion System

Descriptor: Convert existing inquiries into attended consultations.  
Routing cue: For clinics already generating patient demand.

### Regena Growth Partnership

Descriptor: Build and operate the complete patient-growth engine.  
Routing cue: For established clinics that need more demand and better conversion.

### Compare Solutions

Routes to `/solutions`.

The Patient Conversion System appears first and receives the default visual emphasis because it is the lower-barrier entry point and has the strongest current proof. The Growth Partnership receives equal space and premium treatment because many clinics will arrive seeking acquisition support.

The word "Products" is not used in navigation. "Solutions" accurately describes productized managed engagements without implying self-serve software.

All global booking calls to action route to `/book`. Internal page links use real routes rather than homepage anchors.

## 5. Page Designs

### 5.1 Home

The existing homepage remains the executive overview and primary brand expression. It keeps its approved hero, rays, interactive patient journey, capability rail, proof ribbon, infrastructure sections, and booking section.

Required adaptations:

- Update navigation to the multi-page architecture.
- Change anchor-based cross-page links into routes where appropriate.
- Introduce both solutions without turning the homepage into a pricing or package page.
- Present the Patient Conversion System first while preserving meaningful visibility for the Growth Partnership.
- Route the primary call to action to `/book`.

The homepage keeps the strongest cinematic interaction on the site. Other pages use one signature interaction each and do not compete with it.

### 5.2 Solutions Hub

Commercial question: Which Regena engagement fits this clinic?

Sections:

1. Outcome-led hero introducing two paths.
2. Interactive routing split: "Already have demand" versus "Need demand and conversion."
3. Two equal-sized solution panels with the Patient Conversion System first.
4. Side-by-side comparison across clinic situation, core outcome, scope, operating model, and commitment.
5. Shared infrastructure layer showing what both solutions have in common.
6. Clinic-fit guidance.
7. Strategy-call call to action.

Signature interaction: a patient-demand signal branches into the two solutions and reconnects into a shared operating layer.

No public price, "starting at" language, or package totals appear.

### 5.3 Patient Conversion System

Commercial question: How will Regena convert more of the demand the clinic already has?

Sections:

1. Hero focused on the gap between inquiry and attended consultation.
2. Current-state leakage map.
3. Connected conversion system: voice, chat, qualification, scheduling, follow-up, and operating visibility.
4. Patient journey from inquiry through consultation attendance.
5. What Regena builds, operates, and improves.
6. Implementation sequence.
7. VisionMax proof module using only approved claims and marked media placeholders.
8. Best-fit clinic profile and disqualifiers.
9. Expansion path into the Growth Partnership.
10. Strategy-call call to action.

Signature interaction: a scroll-linked conversion pipeline that repairs each broken handoff.

The page leads with business and patient-operation outcomes. Technical architecture appears later for buyers who want deeper validation.

### 5.4 Regena Growth Partnership

Commercial question: How will Regena create and operate a complete growth engine?

Sections:

1. Executive hero connecting acquisition, conversion, and revenue visibility.
2. Demand architecture across up to two acquisition channels in the current base offer.
3. Conversion infrastructure inherited from the Patient Conversion System.
4. Ninety-day operating roadmap.
5. Clear ownership split between Regena and the clinic.
6. Reporting, attribution, creative iteration, and ongoing optimization.
7. Best-fit clinic profile and readiness indicators.
8. Relationship between direct advertising spend and Regena management.
9. Strategy-call call to action.

Signature interaction: an acquisition signal enters the conversion system and resolves into booked consultations and attributed outcomes.

This page is broader and more executive than the Patient Conversion System page. It must not read like a list of marketing deliverables.

### 5.5 Our Approach

Commercial question: What happens after a clinic hires Regena?

The navigation label is "Our Approach," not "How It Works," to distinguish the delivery method from the solution descriptions.

Sections:

1. Hero presenting Regena as an operating partner.
2. Audit: understand the real patient journey and baseline.
3. Architect: define systems, handoffs, scripts, integrations, and measurement.
4. Build: configure and integrate the operating system.
5. Launch: supervised release with quality control.
6. Operate: monitor conversations, workflows, and exceptions.
7. Improve: refine against operational and commercial outcomes.
8. Integration and dashboard overview.
9. Human oversight and accountability model.
10. Strategy-call call to action.

Signature interaction: a scroll-linked architectural blueprint assembles layer by layer.

### 5.6 For Clinics

Commercial question: Is Regena appropriate for this clinic now?

Sections:

1. Hero built around selectivity rather than broad appeal.
2. Clinic situations Regena is designed to solve.
3. Operational readiness: demand, capacity, patient value, access to systems and data, and willingness to improve process.
4. Self-routing between the two solutions.
5. What makes a poor fit.
6. Specialty context for regenerative, longevity, peptide, hormone, and adjacent high-consideration clinics without creating separate SEO pages.
7. Strategy-call call to action.

Signature interaction: a diagnostic matrix maps clinic conditions to the appropriate next step.

The page uses qualification ranges as working fit guidance, not universal industry facts or guarantees.

### 5.7 Outcomes

Commercial question: What evidence supports Regena's approach?

Sections:

1. Proof-led hero.
2. VisionMax result module.
3. Before, system, and after operating narrative.
4. Founder testimonial area labeled as in production until real media is supplied.
5. Outcome categories Regena measures: response, qualification, booking, attendance, and revenue visibility.
6. Methodology note distinguishing observed client results from guarantees.
7. Future case-study slots are omitted rather than shown as empty or fabricated cards.
8. Strategy-call call to action.

Signature interaction: a before-and-after operating timeline tied to the patient signal.

The current 40% VisionMax statement remains draft proof content until its baseline and calculation are confirmed for publication. No additional numeric results are invented.

### 5.9 Book a Strategy Call

Commercial question: Is this worth a focused conversation, and which solution should the call address?

The page is a focused conversion environment with minimal navigation distraction.

Left column:

- Outcome-led headline.
- What the call will accomplish.
- Current proof.
- No-obligation and preparation expectations.

Right column:

#### Step 1 — About You

- Name
- Work email
- Phone
- Clinic name
- Role

#### Step 2 — About the Clinic

- Clinic type
- Number of locations
- Approximate monthly patient inquiries
- Whether the clinic currently runs paid marketing
- Primary bottleneck: conversion, demand and conversion, or unknown
- Current CRM or clinic software, optional

#### Step 3 — Date

Visual calendar selection.

#### Step 4 — Time

Visual time selection and confirmation state.

The UI automatically presents a recommended solution based on the bottleneck answer, but it does not reject or score the applicant publicly.

This build implements the complete local interaction and validation states. It does not transmit personal data or create a real appointment. The production launch remains blocked until a calendar and secure form destination are connected.

Signature interaction: a four-stage progress rail visually connects clinic context to a recommended conversation and booking time.

### 5.10 Privacy and Terms

These pages use a restrained legal-document layout with sticky section navigation on desktop and readable single-column flow on mobile.

The build includes page structure and clearly labeled draft content. It does not claim counsel approval. Production publication requires replacement with approved legal language.

## 6. Visual System

The approved Precision Noir system remains unchanged:

- Warm ivory base.
- Graphite and near-black operating sections.
- Muted mineral green for active system states.
- Restrained violet for completed outcomes and selected moments.
- Cool silver and hairline rules for structure.
- Editorial display typography paired with clear body and monospaced utility text.
- Architectural whitespace and product-interface visualization.

Avoid:

- Generic AI gradients used without structural meaning.
- DNA, leaves, medical crosses, circuit-board motifs, and stock healthcare iconography.
- Floating decorative cards with no information purpose.
- Repeating the homepage hero composition on every page.
- Excessive scroll hijacking or ambient motion.

Each major page receives exactly one signature interaction. Supporting motion uses the existing reveal language. Reduced-motion users receive complete static alternatives with no lost content.

## 7. Reusable Component Architecture

The implementation will introduce focused reusable units rather than duplicating page markup:

- Global site header and responsive navigation.
- Accessible Solutions dropdown and mobile accordion.
- Global footer with solution, company, and legal groups.
- Page hero variants: interface-led, editorial, and conversion.
- Section heading and architectural divider primitives.
- Solution card and solution comparison components.
- Capability rail and system-stage components.
- Clinic-fit and routing components.
- Proof module and result disclaimer.
- Team profile and ownership map components.
- Strategy-call call-to-action component.
- Multi-step booking prototype.

Page-specific content is stored separately from presentation where practical. Shared navigation, solution details, team data, clinic criteria, and page metadata have one typed source of truth.

The current homepage remains intact during extraction. Shared pieces are moved only when required by the new routes, and the final verification must confirm no homepage visual regression.

## 8. Responsive Behavior

Desktop uses wide architectural layouts and page-specific interaction. Tablet reduces column count while preserving hierarchy. Mobile converts complex diagrams into ordered vertical stories.

Requirements:

- No horizontal overflow at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 pixels.
- Touch targets remain at least 44 pixels.
- Solutions dropdown becomes a mobile accordion.
- Comparison tables become stacked comparison groups.
- Multi-step booking remains usable with the mobile keyboard.
- Team profiles become a consistent single-column or two-column grid depending on width.
- Sticky and scroll-linked interactions release into static content under reduced motion and on smaller viewports where appropriate.

## 9. Accessibility and Interaction States

Required behavior:

- Keyboard-operable navigation and dropdown.
- Escape closes open menus.
- Focus returns to the dropdown trigger after closing.
- Current page is exposed with `aria-current`.
- Form controls have persistent labels and inline error messages.
- Booking steps announce progress and validation state.
- Decorative visuals are hidden from assistive technology.
- Meaningful diagrams have concise text equivalents.
- Color is never the only indicator of state.

## 10. Metadata and Internal Linking

Every route receives a unique title, description, canonical path, and social-sharing description. Draft metadata uses buyer-recognizable language and avoids unsupported claims.

Internal links intentionally connect:

- Home to both solutions.
- Each solution to the comparison page and Our Approach.
- For Clinics to the recommended solution.
- Outcomes to the relevant solution.
- Company to Our Approach.
- Every commercial page to `/book`.

The initial build does not add schema claims, review markup, or organization facts that are not verified.

## 11. Testing and Verification

The implementation must include:

- Unit tests for navigation data, route content, solution routing, and booking-step logic.
- Component tests for the Solutions dropdown, mobile navigation, comparison, team profiles, and booking flow.
- Browser tests for every route, navigation path, and primary call to action.
- Responsive overflow checks at 375, 768, 1024, and 1440 pixels.
- Keyboard tests for dropdown and booking flow.
- Reduced-motion checks for every signature interaction.
- Browser-console error checks.
- Production build, lint, and TypeScript verification.
- Desktop, tablet, and mobile screenshot review for every major page.
- A final whole-site pass confirming navigation consistency, design-system fidelity, and no homepage regression.

## 12. Completion Criteria

The design-complete build is finished when:

1. All approved routes render and are reachable through global navigation.
2. The Solutions dropdown clearly routes the two offers.
3. The Patient Conversion System is the default entry without diminishing the Growth Partnership.
4. Every page has a distinct commercial job and signature interaction.
5. Draft copy is credible, buyer-first, and does not invent proof or credentials.
6. The booking experience works locally through all four steps without transmitting data.
7. Team profiles use the confirmed names and roles with non-photographic placeholders.
8. The homepage remains visually and functionally intact.
9. Responsive, accessibility, reduced-motion, lint, test, and production-build checks pass.
10. The remaining launch dependencies are explicitly limited to real integrations, final media, verified proof, headshots, and approved legal copy.
